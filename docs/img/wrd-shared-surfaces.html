A real dashboard leaks into seven surfaces the substrate owns

The toy is a closed loop: your page, your bundle, your verbs. Ship it for real and four things you wrote land in surfaces you did not write, each with a contract. The section numbers say where each contract is spelled out.

what you ship
Your Service CIa node_schema() with a category, commands and requests, mounted on request_graph_ready
Your console surfacea graph canvas with a floating REPL transcript, or a tab of your own
Your bundleJSX importing @wordpress/* and @newspack-nodes/*, built by scripts/build.mjs
Your API credentialswhat a source node needs to fetch
