A record is gated before any request, bounded at the view node before it is published, windowed at the row list before it is drawn, and downsampled at the chart before d3 sees it. Each number is a primitive you inherit by reaching for the shared hook, not a knob to tune afterwards.

Reading the probe model: who owns the metric decides the arithmetic

The source owns it → dedup across co-readers. Two topologies tailing firehose.p0 describe one stream twice: globalMsgRate takes the largest rate per source, probe24hTotals integrates over the union of the readers' windows, oldest end first.
The reader owns it → sum every reader. Backlog and offsetlog cache size are each reader's own debt and cache.
Shown as current → judge by the sample's own timestamp, as backlogTotal does over a 300-second window, because ENTRY_TTL_MS is measured by arrival and a 24-hour replay re-admits a reader that died hours ago as freshly seen.

Two orders that are contracts

The Fetcher connects last. addSliceFetcher wires the receiver Tee to the view first and the Fetcher second: a consumer acting once per answer asks isAsking() as the reply renders, and a settled ask is gone by then. A Tee fans out in connect order.
The one 50 ms interval is the overlay's Overview cards: useOverviewStats samples the page-global IoTelemetry accumulator on a bare setInterval, registered with no Router and invisible to list_timers, because a Router-borne timer would be torn down with the very graph it measures.
