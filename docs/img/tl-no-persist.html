No TM_PERSIST: the drain is the backpressure, and the reader owns its cursor

Tachikoma's ack handshake earns its keep when producer and consumer are decoupled by a queue that can fill. Here every boundary is synchronous and the whole graph drains on one CPU, so an ack has nothing to signal. Null, Tee, Grep, AgeSieve and PayloadTimeout on both sides, plus MemorySieve, which has no counterpart here.

Tachikoma producer, queue, consumer, and a reply for every message
Node.pm answer(), cancel()every node replies to a TM_PERSIST message, one way or the other
Null.pmalso a load generator: fires cached TM_PERSIST payloads while msg_unanswered < max_unanswered
Tee.pmkeeps a TM_PERSIST ledger per fan-out, expired by the [ <timeout> ] argument
Grep, AgeSieve, PayloadTimeoutcancel() a message they refuse, so the producer can tell filtered from delivered
MemorySieve.pmreads a downstream node's output_buffer, or a Topic's pending batches, and cancel()s at the pipeline entry once it reaches max_size
Newspack Nodes one synchronous drain, one CPU, void fill()
Node::fill(): voidno answer(), no cancel(), no max_unanswered. A node cannot observe its sink's disposition; outcomes come back as messages, TO = FROM or TM_ERROR
Null_Nodethe counting black hole alone; the load-generator half is absent, because the window it paced against no longer exists
Tee_Nodeno ledger, no timeout argument: every target is attempted, and the strongest throwable escapes
Grep, Age_Sieve, Value_Timeoutreturn without forwarding, silently (Age_Sieve prints a rate-limited warning only under should_warn); a producer cannot tell a filtered message from a delivered one
MemorySieveno counterpart: it reads what is now a private buffer and makes the very call this decision removed

Where the shape comes back: the browser's sink

dumper-node.js writes each frame into a bounded transcript ring in O(1) and coalesces the expensive publish, the React render and the localStorage persist, onto one requestAnimationFrame flush, announcing the frames it overwrote as a single rate-limited count. A console attached to a firehose Tee degrades into a readable transcript instead of an OOM tab. It needs no cancel(): the drop happens inside the sink, whose cost is not paid in the drain, rather than at the entry to a queue that would otherwise fill.

Do not reintroduce it

The removal is intentional. ADR-3 records the full reasoning and the three conditions that would reopen it; until one of them holds, an ack has nothing to signal and a ledger has nothing to expire.
