A view is the last node of a slice, and every node on the slice is named <subject>:<role>

The subject is what the slice shows; the roles are a fixed set. The shared hooks derive every name from the subject you hand them, and the reply finds the view by address alone: the Fetcher stamps FROM = <subject>:in, the server answers TO = FROM, and nothing correlates.

The subject is the noun, never the verblowercase, hyphenated when compound: worker-status, topology-manager, url-detail; a single word stays one word (sessions, vault). The verb lives on the Fetcher's command argument, so the sessions table is sessions:fetch → sessions:in → sessions:view, not sessions:list:*. A one-shot is the one exception; a hook passing its own scope, because two hooks on one page send the same verb, names a subject — editor-topology, url-lookup — never a third spelling of the verb.

Three routing facts

1
A view is a terminal: no target, no sink. Forwarding out of a view makes it a Tee or a transform; a per-slice merge belongs on the receiver-Tee → view edge, which is what addSliceFetcher's transform slot drops it onto. WorkerStatusTransform is the shipped example.
2
A view never fetches its own data. Upstream sends the verb with FROM = your receiver; the server replies TO = FROM; the receiver Tee fans it to you. A stream view sends nothing either.
3
One slice per view; replies never cross. A verb somebody awaits is minted from its own node and answered there (ADR-7), so nothing reaching a view needs telling apart. A view sitting at counter 0 in the console while its widget renders data is not the node receiving it: look upstream for the god node that is.

A node playing none of the roles is a second slice

The Topology Manager's freshness re-check is freshness:timer beside topology-manager:timer; a partition's segment rail is partition-segments:result with its refresh on partition-rail:timer. It takes a subject of its own rather than a new role.
The roles: :timer the tick, :tee the fan-out, :fetch the Fetcher or Poller, :in the receiver, :transform the one node before the view, :view the view, :result where a one-shot publishes, :link and :stream the two ahead of a stream's view.
The localStorage keys under newspack-nodes: are not node names and follow none of this.
