A Consumer reads one block per tick, commits its cursor as keyframes, and pins a crash by crawling

Durable_Reader owns the cursor, the pump and the checkpoint. Every record advances the cursor past itself; the offsetlog remembers where to resume, how many times this position has been tried, and why the last process stopped.

Stepping off a segment: a single-writer log advances the instant the next segment exists. Under set_multi_writer( true ) the reader waits until the head segment's size has held steady for SEAL_GRACE_SECONDS 2.0, because a peer may append to N for up to DRIFT_RESCAN_INTERVAL_SECONDS 1.0 after N+1 appears; the grace is charged at the head alone, so a backlog replays at full speed.

OFFSETLOG_SEGMENT_SIZE 1 byte makes every frame its own segment, which is what the time-travel debugger's SEEK_FRAME seeks by; do_rotate() adopts the still-empty newest segment on the first commit, so a 1-byte threshold spams no empty segments. Retention keeps 10 keyframes minimum, targets 30, holds anything younger than 300 s, prunes past 900 s, and hard-caps at 60. On boot load_offsetlog() reads the newest segment's last line. An empty offsetlog_dir disables it (the cli's reply-in).

The sink throws — poison, now

The message is dead-lettered with reason throw to :deadletter and the stream keeps moving. No retry: a throw is deterministic per message, so the operator fixes the cause and replays with wp nodes ingest.

A Tail's forward_line() replaces this whole path: a throwing sink propagates out of the drain instead.

A timeout or memory stop — the fair-shot rule

At the handoff cooperative_stop() strikes ONLY when the worker stopped mid-dispatch on the message it BOOTED on, still buffered. An advanced cursor or an empty buffer hands off clean (attempts = 0).

A strike writes the frame with its reason at the unchanged cursor; the successor resumes at attempts + 1. At COOP_MAX_ATTEMPTS 2 the message is quarantined and the cursor advances past it. A memory stop whose fresh baseline was already past 50% blames the process and strikes nothing.

The process died — no throw, no handoff

The frame keeps its last attempts and an EMPTY reason; each boot resumes at attempts + 1. At CRASH_MAX_ATTEMPTS 5 with no reason recorded, resume_attempts_from_frame() enters crawl and arms a one-shot skip of the resumed head.

Crawl: the first drained line — the message in flight when the death struck — is dead-lettered with reason crash; then one line per tick with a checkpoint after each, so the next death pins its culprit. After CHECKPOINT_INTERVAL_S 30 crash-free seconds the reader leaves crawl. assume_clean_shutdown is ignored in crawl.

A clean or an operational stop

shutdown_handoff() sweeps every Shutdown_Sweeper, then checkpoints each durable reader gracefully. A plain Worker_Should_Stop leaves the cursor on the in-flight message, so the successor replays it: at-least-once.

With assume_clean_shutdown on, or when a snapshot node raises Worker_Should_Stop_Clean, the pump commits PAST it. On a FATAL the handoff is skipped, which is what lets a deterministic poison climb toward the crash threshold.
